# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
viewpoints, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
insults, sexualized attention, publication of private information, or other conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behavior.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
